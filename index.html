Roomy

Free up iPhone space privately. Duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos, contacts and calendars — all cleaned on your device.

Common questions

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Empty it there to free the space immediately, or restore anything you deleted by mistake.

How do I get a download link for an archive?

Open the Files app › iCloud Drive › the folder you saved to. Touch and hold the ZIP › Share › Collaborate, set access to “Anyone with the link”, then copy or send the link.

Roomy only sees some of my photos.

Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Roomy › Full Access.

What happens to the Private Vault if I delete the app?

The vault is stored inside the app, so deleting Roomy deletes it. Put items back in Photos first. The vault is included in iPhone backups.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Settings (gear icon on the Clean tab) › Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I undo a contact merge?

Make a backup first with Organize › Back up contacts. iCloud contacts can also be restored from icloud.com › Contacts › Settings.

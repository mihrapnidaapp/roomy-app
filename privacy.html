Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Roomy does not collect any data. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Photos — to find duplicate, similar, blurry and large items, to compress videos, to convert Live Photos and to build ZIP archives. Photos are analyzed on the device and never uploaded by Roomy.
  • Contacts — to find duplicates and tidy phone numbers. Contacts never leave the device.
  • Calendars — to find duplicate and old events. Events never leave the device.
  • Face ID — to lock the Private Vault. Roomy only receives a yes/no answer from iOS; it never sees your face data.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the weekly reminder. The reminder is created on the device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No servers: Roomy does not send your data anywhere.

Files you choose to save

When you save a ZIP archive or a contacts backup, it goes to the location you pick in the Files app (for example iCloud Drive). Storage and sharing of those files are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is processed by Apple. Roomy only receives confirmation that Pro is unlocked; it does not receive your payment details.

Children

Roomy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: Open an issue on GitHub — no account details or photos needed.
